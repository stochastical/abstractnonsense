TIL that [_godspeed_](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Godspeed#English) does not refer to celerity, but rather is derived from the archaic sense of _speed_: ["luck, success, prosperity"](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/speed#English). Which makes the phrase _"Good luck and godspeed"_ a pleonasm! Felix Felicis?
